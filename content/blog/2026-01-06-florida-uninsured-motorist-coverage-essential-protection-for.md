# Florida Uninsured Motorist Coverage: Essential Protection for 2026

Florida drivers face a real uninsured and underinsured driver risk. For Lake City and North Florida families, Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist coverage deserves a careful review.

**Correction updated June 30, 2026:** Florida PIP has not been repealed. HB 1181 died in committee, and PIP remains required under current Florida law.

## What Uninsured Motorist Coverage Protects Against

Uninsured Motorist (UM) and Underinsured Motorist (UIM) coverage can help protect you when another driver:

- Has no insurance.
- Does not have enough coverage.
- Leaves the scene after a hit-and-run.

This coverage can help with injury-related losses that the at-fault driver cannot pay.

## Why UM/UIM Matters in Florida

Florida has a meaningful uninsured-driver exposure. Even when another driver has insurance, low limits may not be enough after a serious accident.

For Lake City drivers, risks include I-75 traffic, rural roads, work vehicles, visiting drivers, and long stretches between services. A serious accident can create costs that exceed basic coverage quickly.

## Florida's Current Insurance Requirements

Current Florida minimum requirements still include:

- **$10,000 Personal Injury Protection (PIP)**
- **$10,000 Property Damage Liability (PDL)**

PIP remains required for most Florida-registered vehicles with four or more wheels. UM/UIM coverage is not required, but insurance companies must offer it and you must reject it in writing if you do not want it.

## The 2026 PIP-Repeal Rumor

Some online summaries have assigned PIP a July 2026 termination date. That is not correct under current law.

HB 1181 was a proposed bill, not an enacted law. The official Florida Senate bill page lists HB 1181 as **Died in Judiciary Committee**.

UM/UIM remains important, but not because PIP is ending in July 2026. It is important because uninsured and underinsured drivers create real financial risk.

## How Much UM/UIM Should You Consider?

Many drivers choose UM/UIM limits that match their Bodily Injury Liability limits. Families with multiple vehicles may also compare stacked versus non-stacked UM coverage.

The right choice depends on:

- Drivers and vehicles in the household.
- Current liability limits.
- Assets and income to protect.
- Driving patterns and commute.
- Budget and risk tolerance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is UM required in Florida?

No. It is optional, but insurers must offer it. If you reject it, the rejection must be documented.

### Should I reject UM coverage?

Do not reject it casually. UM/UIM can be valuable protection when the other driver has no coverage or insufficient coverage.

### Does the failed PIP repeal make UM less important?

No. UM/UIM is still important to review because Florida's road risk and low minimum requirements can leave gaps.
