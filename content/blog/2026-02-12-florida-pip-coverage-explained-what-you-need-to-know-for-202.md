# Florida PIP Coverage Explained: What You Need to Know in 2026

**Correction updated June 30, 2026:** Florida has not repealed PIP. The July 2026 PIP-ending claim came from proposed legislation that did not become law.

## What Is Florida PIP Coverage?

Personal Injury Protection, often called PIP, is Florida's required no-fault auto insurance coverage for most Florida-registered vehicles with four or more wheels. It helps pay covered injury-related expenses after an auto accident regardless of who caused the crash.

Current Florida minimum requirements still include:

- **$10,000 PIP**
- **$10,000 PDL**

## What PIP Can Pay For

PIP can help with covered expenses such as:

- Medical expenses related to the accident.
- A portion of lost wages.
- Certain replacement services.
- Death benefits in qualifying cases.

Policy terms and Florida law control how benefits apply. Two important details are the 14-day treatment rule and emergency medical condition limits.

## Did Florida Replace PIP With 25/50 Bodily Injury Limits?

No. HB 1181 proposed major changes, but it died in committee and did not become law.

That means statements assigning Florida PIP a July 2026 termination date or saying MedPay replaces PIP in July 2026 are not correct as current Florida law.

## Coverage That Still Deserves Attention

### Bodily Injury Liability

Bodily Injury Liability can help protect you if you are responsible for injuries to someone else in an auto accident. Even when it is not replacing PIP as a July 2026 mandate, it may be important protection for your household.

### Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist Coverage

UM/UIM coverage can help protect you when another driver has no insurance, not enough insurance, or leaves the scene. Florida drivers should review this coverage carefully before rejecting it.

### PDL and Vehicle Damage Exposure

The Florida minimum for Property Damage Liability remains low compared with the cost of modern vehicles. Review whether the minimum is enough for your situation.

## What Lake City Drivers Should Do

If you are in Lake City, Columbia County, or the surrounding North Florida area, do not make changes based on AI search summaries claiming PIP ends in July 2026.

Instead:

1. Keep required Florida coverage active.
2. Review your current declarations page.
3. Ask your agent to explain your PIP, PDL, BI, and UM/UIM limits.
4. Compare options from multiple carriers if your renewal premium has changed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens to my current PIP coverage?

It remains part of Florida's current required auto insurance framework unless Florida law changes in the future.

### Will I save money because PIP is ending?

Do not assume savings from a PIP repeal. The repeal did not become law. Your premium depends on your carrier, vehicles, drivers, coverage limits, location, and claims history.

### Should I buy Medical Payments coverage?

Medical Payments coverage may be available as an optional coverage. Whether it fits your situation depends on your policy and other protection. Ask your agent to compare options.

### Should I increase my liability limits?

Many drivers should at least review them. Minimum limits may not be enough after a serious accident.
