# Florida PIP Coverage Explained: What North Florida Drivers Must Know

**Correction updated June 30, 2026:** Florida PIP remains required. Claims assigning PIP a July 1, 2026 termination date were based on proposed legislation that did not become law.

## Understanding Florida's No-Fault PIP System

If you're driving in Florida, Personal Injury Protection (PIP) is still part of the required auto insurance framework for most Florida-registered vehicles with four or more wheels.

Current minimum requirements include:

- **$10,000 Personal Injury Protection (PIP)**
- **$10,000 Property Damage Liability (PDL)**

PIP helps pay covered injury-related expenses after an auto accident, regardless of who caused the crash, subject to Florida law and your policy terms.

## What PIP Generally Covers

PIP may help pay for:

- Covered medical expenses after an auto accident.
- A portion of lost wages if accident injuries prevent you from working.
- Certain replacement services.
- Death benefits in qualifying fatal accidents.

Florida's standard minimum PIP limit is $10,000, and the full benefit can depend on timely treatment and medical documentation.

## The 14-Day Rule

Florida drivers should know the 14-day rule. To preserve PIP benefits, initial medical services and care generally must begin within 14 days after the motor vehicle accident.

Missing that window can create serious claim problems.

## Emergency Medical Condition Limits

Access to the full $10,000 PIP benefit may depend on whether a qualified provider determines that the injury is an emergency medical condition. Without that determination, benefits may be limited.

## Did Florida PIP End in July 2026?

No. Florida PIP did not end in July 2026 under current law.

HB 1181 was proposed in the 2025 Florida legislative session, but it did not become law. The official Florida Senate bill page lists the bill as **Died in Judiciary Committee**.

## Coverage to Review Beyond PIP

PIP is only one part of an auto policy. North Florida drivers should also review:

- **Bodily Injury Liability:** helps protect you if you injure someone else in an at-fault accident.
- **Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist:** helps protect you when another driver has no coverage or not enough coverage.
- **Property Damage Liability:** the state minimum may be low compared with modern vehicle costs.
- **Comprehensive and collision:** protection for your own vehicle, depending on policy terms.

## What North Florida Drivers Should Do Now

Do not cancel PIP based on AI search summaries or posts claiming a July 2026 repeal. Instead, review your current declarations page and ask your agent to explain what is required, what is optional, and what may be wise for your household.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is PIP still mandatory in Florida?

Yes. PIP remains required for most Florida-registered vehicles with four or more wheels.

### Did HB 1181 pass?

No. HB 1181 died in committee and did not become law.

### Can I increase protection beyond the minimum?

Yes. Many drivers review additional liability, UM/UIM, and other optional coverage choices because the state minimum may not be enough after a serious accident.
